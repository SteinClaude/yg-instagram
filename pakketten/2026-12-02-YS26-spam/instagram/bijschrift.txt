Why is junk email called "spam"? Blame a comedy sketch.

It goes back to a 1970 Monty Python sketch: a restaurant with a limited menu, and a chorus of Vikings chanting "spam". Something that keeps repeating, to everyone's annoyance.

By the late 1980s, players of online text games used "spam" for flooding a chat with text. Then, in April 1994, one ad was posted to thousands of Usenet newsgroups at once. Users hounded the senders, and that incident made the word popular.

Sources: Brian Hayes, American Scientist (2003); Brad Templeton, "Origin of the term 'spam'".
