## Curt Wohleber, "The Shopping Cart", Invention & Technology 20(1), zomer 2004
https://www.inventionandtech.com/node/86507

- Goldman bezat "a chain of Humpty Dumpty supermarkets based in Oklahoma City"; het idee kwam van een klapstoel
- prototype "designed to carry two 19-by13-by-9-inch wire baskets"; karren ingevoerd "in the summer of 1937"
- Goldman "hired men and women of assorted ages to pose as customers and use the carts."
- Orla Watson vroeg in sept 1946 een patent aan op een nestbare kar; in 1949 "agreed to license Watson's patent"

## CBS News, "Almanac: The first shopping cart" (4 juni 2017)
https://www.cbsnews.com/news/almanac-the-first-shopping-cart

- op 4 juni 1937 rolde Goldman "the world's first shopping cart at his Humpty Dumpty supermarket chain in Oklahoma City" uit
- "a basket on the seat of a folding chair, and wheels on the legs"

## NPR (Mallory Yu), "The history of the shopping cart" (29 maart 2025, via HPPR)
https://www.hppr.org/2025-03-29/the-history-of-the-shopping-cart

- "No one wanted them."
- "he hired models to walk around his stores with the carts"

## Encyclopedia of Oklahoma History and Culture, "Goldman, Sylvan Nathan (1898–1984)"
https://www.okhistory.org/publications/enc/entry?entry=GO004

- "in 1934 Sylvan purchased the faltering Humpty-Dumpty grocery chain."
- "His greatest contribution was the shopping cart, invented in 1936 and patented by Goldman. He established the Folding Basket Carrier Company to manufacture his invention."

## Beeld, letters en geluid
- Beeld: eigen animatie (SVG, gereedschap/short.cjs, versie 2). Geen stockbeeld, geen beeld van anderen, geen AI-beeld.
- Letters: Instrument Sans en IBM Plex Mono, SIL Open Font License (gereedschap/short/fonts/).
- Geluid: eigen geluidseffecten (whoosh, tik, klap, dreun, stijgende toon, bel, plop, rol), zelf gesynthetiseerd met ffmpeg uit sinus en ruis (gereedschap/short/geluid.cjs). Stem: Engelse AI-stem Kokoro-82M (open source, Apache-2.0, stem "fenrir"), lokaal gemaakt; ingesproken tekst in bron/stem.txt. Geen muziek, geen audio van anderen.
