The first shoppers refused to use shopping carts.

Oklahoma City grocer Sylvan Goldman started with a folding chair: a basket on the seat, wheels on the legs. The final version carried two wire baskets.

On 4 June 1937 he rolled them out in his stores. Nobody wanted them. So Goldman hired people to pose as customers and push the carts around. He patented the cart and set up a company to make it.

Sources: Invention & Technology, "The Shopping Cart" (2004); CBS News (2017); NPR (2025); Encyclopedia of Oklahoma History and Culture.
