Monopoly began as a game against landlords.

In 1904, Lizzie Magie patented The Landlord's Game. It spread the ideas of reformer Henry George, and it was meant to show the tragic effects of land-grabbing and the concentration of wealth.

During the Great Depression, Charles Darrow got the game through a friend, made copies at home and sold it to Parker Brothers as his own. They bought the rights in 1935, and sales skyrocketed.

Sources: Lizzie J. Magie, US Patent 748,626; The Strong National Museum of Play; NPR (2015); Mental Floss (2013).
