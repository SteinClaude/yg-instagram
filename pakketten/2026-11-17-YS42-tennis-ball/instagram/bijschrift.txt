Why are tennis balls yellow?

They used to be white, or even black, depending on the colour of the court. In 1972 yellow balls went into the rules of tennis, because research showed they were easier to see for television viewers.

Wimbledon kept its white balls until 1986. They were getting stained green on the grass.

Sources: International Tennis Federation, "History of Tennis Balls"; The Associated Press (2024).
