The microwave oven was discovered by a melted candy bar.

Engineer Percy Spencer was standing next to a magnetron, a tube that produces microwave energy, when the candy bar in his pocket started to melt. (His grandson says it was a peanut cluster bar, not chocolate.)

Then he tried popcorn: it popped all over his lab. Eggs exploded. Spencer filed a patent in 1945. The first Radarange, in 1947, stood about 6 feet tall and weighed around 750 pounds.

Sources: Lemelson-MIT; IEEE Spectrum (2016); Linda Hall Library (2019); Popular Mechanics (2022).
