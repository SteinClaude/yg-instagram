Titel (37/100 tekens):
Why do Post-it Notes only half stick?

Beschrijving:
The glue behind Post-it Notes almost stayed on a shelf. It grabbed, but it let go, and for years nobody had the right use for it.

What the sources say:
- 1968: Spencer Silver, a senior scientist at 3M, discovered an acrylic adhesive with unique properties.
- It was made of tiny spheres, giving "a high level of tack but a low degree of adhesion".
- Silver wanted to market it as a spray or as a surface for bulletin boards; it "might have been consigned to a shelf indefinitely".
- Art Fry, singing in his church choir, was frustrated that the paper bookmarks in his hymnal kept slipping out of place.
- Fry coated paper with the adhesive and made repositionable notes.
- 1980: Post-it Notes were introduced nationally in the United States.
