Velcro was invented on a walk with a dog

Beschrijving:
In 1941, George de Mestral came home from a walk with burrs stuck to his pants and his dog's fur. He looked at one under a microscope.

What the source says:
- 1941: George de Mestral took a walk in the woods with his dog, an Irish Pointer.
- Burrs from burdock plants clung to his pants and his dog's fur.
- At home he examined a burr under a microscope: it was covered in thousands of tiny hooks that gripped the looped threads of his clothing.
- He made the hooks from nylon, working with a fabric manufacturer in Lyon, France, and patented the hook and loop fastener.
- The name combines the French words velour (velvet) and crochet (hooks).

Source:
