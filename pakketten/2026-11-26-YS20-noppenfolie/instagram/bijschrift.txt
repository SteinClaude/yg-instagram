Bubble wrap was supposed to be wallpaper.

In 1957, in a garage in New Jersey, engineers Alfred Fielding and Marc Chavannes sealed two plastic sheets together with air bubbles trapped inside. The plan: textured wallpaper. It didn't take hold. They also tried greenhouses, as cheap insulation.

Its real purpose turned out to be packaging. In 1959, IBM launched its new 1401 computer, a marketer pitched the material to IBM, and bubble wrap finally had its use.

Sources: Sealed Air press release (2026, via Nasdaq); Invention & Technology, "Bubble Wrap: From Wallpaper To Stress Reliever" (2020).
