## Sealed Air, persbericht (26 januari 2026, via Nasdaq)
https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/sealed-air-celebrates-innovation-and-its-iconic-brand-national-bubble-wrapr

- "The story of BUBBLE WRAP® brand packaging began in 1957 in Hawthorne, New Jersey, when engineers Alfred W. Fielding and Marc Chavannes attempted to create a textured wall covering by laminating two plastic sheets with air bubbles trapped inside."
- "While the wallpaper concept did not take hold, their invention found its true purpose in protective packaging, leading to the founding of Sealed Air Corporation in 1960."

## Emily Prokop, "Bubble Wrap: From Wallpaper To Stress Reliever", Invention & Technology (2020)
https://www.inventionandtech.com/node/87109

- "In 1957, Alfred W Fielding and Marc Chavannes wanted to create a high-end plastic wallpaper."
- "In Fielding's garage, they sealed two plastic sheets together, creating air pockets trapped inside"
- "They first tried selling it to greenhouses as a sort of cheap insulation, but it was difficult to market plastic for walls."
- "IBM premiered its new 1401 variable-word-length computer in 1959"
- "Frederick Bowers, a marketer at Sealed Air, pitched the material to IBM, and finally a use was found for Bubble Wrap."

## Beeld, letters en geluid
- Beeld: eigen animatie (SVG, gereedschap/short.cjs, versie 2). Geen stockbeeld, geen beeld van anderen, geen AI-beeld.
- Letters: Instrument Sans en IBM Plex Mono, SIL Open Font License (gereedschap/short/fonts/).
- Geluid: eigen geluidseffecten (whoosh, tik, klap, dreun, stijgende toon, bel, plop, rol), zelf gesynthetiseerd met ffmpeg uit sinus en ruis (gereedschap/short/geluid.cjs).
