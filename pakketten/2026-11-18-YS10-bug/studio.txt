This moth was the first "actual" computer bug. The word is older

Beschrijving:
In 1947 a team at Harvard found a moth inside their computer and taped it into the logbook. The joke only works because "bug" already meant a fault.

What the sources say:
- September 9, 1947: a moth was found caught between the relay contacts of the Harvard Mark II computer in Cambridge, Massachusetts.
- The team taped the dead moth into the logbook, with the note: "First actual case of bug being found."
- "Bug" for a fault was much older: in an 1878 letter, Thomas Edison wrote about "'Bugs' as such little faults and difficulties are called."
- Grace Hopper was present and later made the incident famous in her many public lectures.
- The logbook, with the moth, is today in the collection of the Smithsonian's National Museum of American History.
