Titel (51/100 tekens):
Why your PIN has 4 digits (it was supposed to be 6)

Beschrijving:
The inventor of the cash machine planned a six-digit PIN. Then his wife told him six was too many.

What the source says:
- 27 June 1967: the first cash machine was installed at a Barclays branch in a north London suburb.
- Inventor John Shepherd-Barron got the idea from a chocolate bar dispenser, "replacing chocolate with cash".
- There were no plastic bank cards yet: the machine used special cheques coded with a mildly radioactive material.
- He first planned six-digit PINs, but cut them to four after his wife said six was too many. In his words: "she could only remember four figures, so because of her, four figures became the world standard."
- By 2010 there were at least 1.7 million cash machines worldwide.
