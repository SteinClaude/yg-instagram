Why does your PIN have 4 digits? It was supposed to be 6.

The first cash machine opened in 1967 at a Barclays branch in north London. Its inventor, John Shepherd-Barron, got the idea from a chocolate bar dispenser. There were no plastic bank cards yet: the machine took special cheques.

He planned a six-digit PIN. His wife said six was too many: she could only remember four. So four became the world standard.

Source: Al Jazeera English, "Cash machine inventor dies at 84" (2010).
