Titel (41/100 tekens):
Why is there an @ in every email address?

Beschrijving:
In 1971, engineer Ray Tomlinson was building an early email system and needed a way to separate the user from the machine. He picked a symbol that was rarely used in computing.

What the sources say:
- Ray Tomlinson worked at Bolt, Beranek and Newman (BBN) when he developed an early email system; the Internet Hall of Fame dates the system to 1971.
- He first sent messages between two computers in his office, with keyboards about 10 feet apart.
- He chose @ to separate user names from machine names, because it was rarely used in computing at the time. In his words: "It's the only preposition on the keyboard."
- About the first email he said: "The first email is completely forgettable." The message itself is forgotten.
