The $ sign is probably not an S. It's two letters.

Where does the dollar sign come from? Even the US Bureau of Engraving and Printing says its origin "has been variously accounted for". The most accepted explanation: "Ps", short for the Spanish peso.

Old manuscripts show the S gradually being written over the P, until it looked almost exactly like $. And the sign was widely used before the United States adopted the dollar in 1785.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Engraving and Printing, "Currency FAQs".
