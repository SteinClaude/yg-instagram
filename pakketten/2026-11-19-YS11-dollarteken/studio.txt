Where does the dollar sign come from? The most accepted explanation: the abbreviation "Ps" for the Spanish peso, with the S written over the P.

What the source says:
- The U.S. Bureau of Engraving and Printing says the origin of the $ sign "has been variously accounted for".
- The most commonly accepted explanation: it evolved, independently in different places, from the Mexican or Spanish "Ps" for pesos.
- From a study of old manuscripts: the S gradually came to be written over the P, developing a close equivalent of the $ mark.
- The sign was widely used before the United States adopted the dollar in 1785.
