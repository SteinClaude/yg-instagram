Titel (70/100 tekens):
Why does a phone keypad start with 1-2-3, but a calculator with 7-8-9?

Beschrijving:
Before the push-button phone, Bell engineers tested sixteen keypad layouts. The adding-machine layout lost, by a fraction of a second.

What the sources say:
- A Bell System Technical Journal study (R. L. Deininger, July 1960) compared keypad layouts for the push-button telephone. "Sixteen different arrangements were selected for the first part of this study."
- The arrangement frequently found in ten-key adding machines "was not the best of the first three arrangements compared."
- The same geometric configuration with a different numbering scheme was superior in keying performance, but the differences were small: 5.08 seconds against 4.92 seconds.
- The telephone keypad got "1-2-3" on the top row instead of the bottom, as on a calculator; it sprang from research conducted or overseen by Bell Labs psychologist John E. Karlin (Boston Globe).
- The study does not say why adding machines put 7-8-9 on top.

Sources:
R. L. Deininger, "Human Factors Engineering Studies of the Design and Use of Pushbutton Telephone Sets", Bell System Technical Journal 39(4), July 1960 (scan): https://www.vcalc.net/Documents/touchtone_hf.pdf
