The first :-) was invented because of a fake warning.

September 1982, a message board at Carnegie Mellon University. A joke post warned that an elevator was "contaminated with mercury". Many people took it seriously. Others suggested joke markers: a star, a percent sign.

Then, on 19 September, Scott Fahlman proposed :-) "Read it sideways." And for serious posts: :-( His post was lost for years, until it was found on an old backup tape in 2002.

Sources: the original 1982 message board thread and "Smiley Lore" (Scott Fahlman, Carnegie Mellon University); The Register (2002).
