Why your windshield cracks but doesn't shatter.

In 1903, French chemist Édouard Bénédictus dropped a glass beaker. It broke, but it didn't splinter: the solution it had held had left a thin film inside that kept the pieces together.

He patented laminated glass in 1909. In World War I it was needed for gas mask lenses and aviator goggles, and it started being used in windshields.

Sources: VILLUM Window Collection, "Édouard Bénédictus"; American Ceramic Society Bulletin (2025).
