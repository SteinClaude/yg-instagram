Why is a marathon exactly 42.195 km (26.2 miles)?

The odd distance comes from one race: the 1908 Olympic marathon in London. It started by Windsor Castle and finished in front of the Royal Box in the White City Stadium: precisely 26 miles, 385 yards.

You may have heard the start was chosen for the royal children. World Athletics tells that story; Olympedia calls it "certainly false". Either way, in 1921 the 1908 distance became the standard.

Sources: Olympedia, "Marathon, Men" (1908); World Athletics, "Marathon"; Mental Floss (2018).
