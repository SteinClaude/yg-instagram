Why do your jeans have that tiny pocket?

It is called a watch pocket: it was made to hold a pocket watch, a typical thing to carry in the late 1800s.

The riveted pockets were patented in 1873, and the original pants had four pockets, the little one included. The "fifth pocket" is actually the back left one, which only showed up in 1901.

Sources: Levi Strauss & Co. (2017); US Patent No. 139,121 (1873).
