Why do barber poles have red and white stripes?

Beschrijving:
That cheerful red and white pole outside the barber's has a bloody backstory. Centuries ago, barbers didn't just cut hair: they also pulled teeth and let blood. The story goes that bloody bandages hung on a pole by the door and twisted in the wind into red and white stripes. In 1745, the surgeons in England split from the barbers. The pole stayed.

What the sources say:
- Barbers also did "bloodletting, pulled teeth". (TheCollector)
- 1745: "the Surgeons left the Company". (The Worshipful Company of Barbers)
- The bandages on the pole are a story that is often told, not a proven fact; the sources differ on the details.
