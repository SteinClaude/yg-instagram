Why do barber poles have red and white stripes?

That cheerful pole outside the barber's has a bloody backstory. Centuries ago, barbers didn't just cut hair: they also pulled teeth and let blood. The story goes that bloody bandages hung on a pole by the door and twisted in the wind into stripes. In 1745, England's surgeons split from the barbers. The pole stayed.

Sources: The Worshipful Company of Barbers; TheCollector; Kearny County Museum.
