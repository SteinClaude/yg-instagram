Why is "pound" written "lb"? There's no L or B in it.

It comes from Latin. The Romans said "libra pondo": a pound by weight. English took the spoken word "pound" from pondo, and the abbreviation from libra.

Libra first meant balance or scales, as in the star sign. A Roman pound weighed about 327 grams. And the £ sign? That's an L for libra too.

Sources: Dictionary.com; Arika Okrent, Mental Floss (2014); Royal Mint Museum.
