1. Why is pound written L-B? There's no L or B in pound.
2. It's Latin. Libra.
3. Libra first meant a balance, or scales. Like the star sign.
4. The Romans said libra pondo: a pound by weight.
5. English kept pondo for speaking. It became pound.
6. But for writing, it kept libra. L-B.
7. A Roman libra weighed about 327 grams.
8. And the pound sign? Also libra. It grew out of the letter L.
9. Pound for saying it. Libra for writing it.
