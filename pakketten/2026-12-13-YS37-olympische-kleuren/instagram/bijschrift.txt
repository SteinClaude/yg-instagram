Why the Olympic rings have exactly those colours.

In August 1913, Pierre de Coubertin presented the five rings, for five parts of the world: blue, yellow, black, green and red, on a white background.

Those six colours together, he wrote, "reproduce the colors of all the nations, with no exception." The flag first flew in 1914 in Alexandria, Egypt, and at the Olympic Games in 1920, in Antwerp.

Source: Olympedia, "Olympic Flag" (quoting Coubertin, 1913).
