Why are school buses yellow?

Before 1939, school buses had no standard colour. Then professor Frank Cyr gathered experts to set national rules for school buses. They lined up shades from lemon yellow to orange-red along a wall, and picked the one that is easiest to see in the early morning and evening, exactly when buses drive.

Its official name: National School Bus Chrome.

Sources: Mental Floss (2013); The Children's Museum of Indianapolis (2020).
