Why is the emergency number 999? It started with a fire

Beschrijving:
The world's first emergency number came from London in 1937, after a fire two years earlier.

What the source says:
- In November 1935, a fire on Wimpole Street in London killed five women.
- A review of that fire led to the Belgrave Committee, set up to study how telephone operators could identify emergency calls.
- It recommended a single number to alert the emergency services, one that must be easy to remember. The answer: 999.
- The first ever 999 call was made in London on 30 June 1937, the first service of its kind in the world.
- In the first week, 1,336 calls were made.

Source:
