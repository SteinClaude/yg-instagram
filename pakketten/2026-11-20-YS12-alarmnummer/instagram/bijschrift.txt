Why is the emergency number 999? It started with a fire.

In November 1935, a fire on Wimpole Street in London killed five women. The review that followed led to a committee on how telephone operators could recognise emergency calls.

Its advice: one number for the emergency services, and it had to be easy to remember. The answer was 999. The first 999 call was made in London on 30 June 1937, the first service of its kind in the world. In the first week, 1,336 calls came in.

Source: Greater London Authority, City Hall blog, "999 celebrates its 77th birthday".
