The recycling symbol was a student's contest entry.

In 1970, the year of the first Earth Day, a cardboard company held a student design contest for a recycling symbol. Gary Anderson, a 23-year-old student at the University of Southern California, entered. He was inspired by the cyclic nature of a Möbius strip.

He won $2,500. And the symbol went into the public domain: free for everyone to use. That's why you see it everywhere.

Sources: The Long + Short (2014); Aspen Journalism (2024); Baltimore Banner (2026).
