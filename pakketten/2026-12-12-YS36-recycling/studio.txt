The recycling symbol was a student's contest entry

Beschrijving:
In 1970, a cardboard company held a design contest for a recycling symbol. A 23-year-old student, Gary Anderson, won it.

What the sources say:
- In 1970, the year of the first Earth Day, the Container Corporation of America, "a large cardboard manufacturer, held a student design contest to create a symbol representing the recycling process" (The Long + Short; Aspen Journalism).
- Gary Anderson was "a 23-year-old student at the University of Southern California" (The Long + Short; Baltimore Banner).
- "Anderson was inspired by the cyclic nature of a Möbius strip" (The Long + Short).
- "He won $2,500." (The Long + Short); Aspen Journalism: a $2,500 tuition grant and a trip to the conference.
- The symbol would be "free to use in the public domain" (Baltimore Banner).
