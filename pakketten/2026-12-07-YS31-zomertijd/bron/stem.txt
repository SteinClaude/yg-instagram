1. Twice a year we move the clocks. It started with a war.
2. Not with Benjamin Franklin. In 1784 he wrote a joke: fire cannons in the streets to wake people up.
3. He never invented moving the clocks.
4. 1895, New Zealand. Insect collector George Hudson wants more daylight after work. Move the clocks two hours each spring.
5. The local science society ridiculed his idea.
6. 1907, London. William Willett pays for a pamphlet himself: The Waste of Daylight.
7. Then the war. April 30, 1916: Germany and Austria-Hungary move the clocks first. To save coal.
8. Britain follows: its law passes on May 17, 1916.
9. You still change your clock for a wartime idea.
