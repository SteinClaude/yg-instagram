The world's first traffic light exploded

Beschrijving:
In December 1868 London got the first traffic light, outside the Houses of Parliament. The idea came from railway signals.

What the sources say:
- December 1868: the world's first traffic lights were installed in Westminster, London, outside the Houses of Parliament.
- They were designed by J P Knight, a railway signalling engineer, who took the idea from the railway signalling system onto roads.
- By day, semaphore arms directed the traffic; at night, red and green gas lamps. All of it was operated by a police constable.
- A leak in the gas line made one of the lights explode, seriously burning the policeman on duty, and the trial was scrapped.
- Traffic lights disappeared from the UK for more than 40 years; electric signals became common in London around 1929.
