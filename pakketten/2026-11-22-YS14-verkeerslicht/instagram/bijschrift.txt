The world's first traffic light exploded.

In December 1868, London got the first traffic lights, outside the Houses of Parliament. Railway signalling engineer J P Knight took the idea from the railways: semaphore arms by day, red and green gas lamps at night, all worked by a police constable.

Then a leak in the gas line made one of the lights explode, seriously burning the policeman on duty. The trial was scrapped, and traffic lights disappeared from the UK for more than 40 years.

Sources: PA Media, "150 years since world's first traffic light in London" (2018); History Hit, "Where Were the World's First Traffic Lights?".
