## PA Media (via Yahoo News UK), "Ready, steady, go: 150 years since world's first traffic light in London" (december 2018)
https://uk.news.yahoo.com/ready-steady-150-years-since-091344126.html

- geplaatst op de kruising van Great George Street en Bridge Street in Westminster, 9 december 1868
- "John Peake Knight took the idea from the railway signalling system onto roads"; "red and green gas-powered lamps"
- een lamp ontplofte, "seriously burning the policeman on duty"; sommige verhalen zeggen dat hij stierf; de proef werd gestopt
- verdwenen uit het VK "for more than 40 odd years"; "around 1929 the first electric signals started becoming commonplace in London"

## History Hit, "Where Were the World's First Traffic Lights?"
https://www.historyhit.com/1868-first-traffic-light/

- 10 december 1868, "outside the Houses of Parliament in London"
- "designed by J P Knight, a railway signalling engineer"
- "semaphore arms to direct the traffic during the day, and red and green gas lamps at night, all operated by a police constable"
- "A leak in the gas line caused them to explode"

## Wat bewust niet gezegd is
- De plan-bron (Londonist) gaf op 2 nov een 403; daarom PA en History Hit. De bronnen verschillen over de dag (9 of 10 december) en over of de agent stierf: de video zegt "December 1868" en "seriously burned" (wat beide bronnen dragen). Niet gezegd: wat rood en groen precies betekenden (geen van beide bronnen zegt het), de datum van de ontploffing.
