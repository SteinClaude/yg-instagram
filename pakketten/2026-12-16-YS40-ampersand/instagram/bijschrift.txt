Why is "&" called an ampersand?

The & sign was once the 27th member of the alphabet. It was formed from the letters in "et", the Latin word for "and".

Because "X, Y, Z, and" sounded confusing, students said "and per se and"; per se means "by itself". Over time that phrase was slurred together into one word: ampersand.

Sources: Dictionary.com (2020); The Saturday Evening Post (2021).
