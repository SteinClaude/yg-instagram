Sliced bread was invented by a jeweller.

Otto Rohwedder ran three jewellery stores in Missouri. In late 1917, a fire destroyed the blueprints of his bread slicer. Sliced bread goes stale, so by 1927 his machine sliced the bread and also wrapped it.

In 1928, a bakery in Chillicothe, Missouri, bought his first machine, and on 7 July it sold the first loaf of sliced bread. In 1943, during World War II, the US even banned sliced bread, for seven weeks.

Sources: Lemelson-MIT, "Otto Rohwedder"; Mental Floss (2019).
