## The Local Denmark, "Why do Danes cover each other in cinnamon on their 25th birthdays?" (Elizabeth Anne Brown, 18 feb 2022)
https://www.thelocal.dk/20220218/why-do-danes-cover-each-other-in-cinnamon-on-their-25th-birthdays

- "don't be alarmed if you see a young person tied to a lamppost and smothered in cinnamon"
- "While unwed 25-year-olds can look forward to a cinnamon shower"; een set met "duct tape to affix the celebrant to a lamppost"
- "the Danish tradition of the pebersvend, or pebermø for women"; "most closely approximated in English as 'pepper dude.'"
- "The tradition's earliest roots date back to the 15th century"; "Young bachelors went traveling to trade spices around the world"; "They would never stay in a place long enough to get married"
- "by the 1980s mass media ensured that the idea had spread to the whole of Denmark"
- "Danes who still aren't hitched by 30 may receive a cheeky pepper and salt mill set as a birthday present"
- "people actually having been endangered by inhaling all of this cinnamon"

## Wat bewust niet gezegd is
- Niet gezegd: water of eieren om de kaneel te laten plakken (niet in de bron), dat 30-jarigen met peper worden bespoten (de bron zegt "at least according to the companies selling pepper cannons": te zwak), een vertaling van "pebermø" (de bron geeft die niet). Op beeld 1 staan een café en auto's; die vallen buiten de uitsnede. De lantaarnpaal en de vallende kaneel zijn eigen animatie.
