Why is November month 11 if its name means "nine"?

November comes from "novem", the Latin word for nine. In the early Roman calendar it really was the ninth month: according to tradition, that calendar had only ten months and started in March.

Then two months were added, January and February. The old names stayed, but the numbers no longer fit. The same goes for September (seven), October (eight) and December (ten).

Sources: timeanddate.com, "The Roman Calendar"; The Old Farmer's Almanac (2026).
