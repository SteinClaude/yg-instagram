## Science History Institute, "Roy J. Plunkett"
https://www.sciencehistory.org/education/scientific-biographies/roy-j-plunkett/

- "Teflon, discovered by Roy J. Plunkett (1910–1994) at the DuPont Company's Jackson Laboratory in 1938"
- "Plunkett's first assignment at DuPont was researching new chlorofluorocarbon refrigerants"
- "a hundred pounds of tetrafluoroethylene gas (TFE) and stored it in small cylinders"
- "none of the gas came out—yet the cylinder weighed the same as before"; "They opened it and found a white powder"
- "TFE had polymerized to produce this substance"
- "heat resistant and chemically inert"; "very low surface friction so that most other substances would not adhere to it"
- "Its first use was fulfilling the requirements of the gaseous diffusion process of the Manhattan Project"; "Teflon pots and pans were invented years later"
- "was an accidental invention—unlike most of the other polymer products"

## Beeld, letters en geluid
- Beeld: eigen animatie (SVG, gereedschap/short.cjs, versie 2). Geen stockbeeld, geen beeld van anderen, geen AI-beeld.
- Letters: Instrument Sans en IBM Plex Mono, SIL Open Font License (gereedschap/short/fonts/).
- Geluid: eigen geluidseffecten (whoosh, tik, klap, dreun, stijgende toon, bel, plop, rol), zelf gesynthetiseerd met ffmpeg uit sinus en ruis (gereedschap/short/geluid.cjs). Stem: Engelse AI-stem Kokoro-82M (open source, Apache-2.0, stem "fenrir"), lokaal gemaakt; ingesproken tekst in bron/stem.txt. Geen muziek, geen audio van anderen.
- Controle: bron/controle-beweging.jpg (beelden uit de mp4, midden in de bewegingen).
