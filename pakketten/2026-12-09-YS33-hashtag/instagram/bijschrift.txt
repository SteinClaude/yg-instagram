Twitter said this symbol would never catch on.

In August 2007, Chris Messina asked: how about using # for groups? Chat rooms on IRC already used it to name their channels. Twitter thought it looked clunky.

Then, in October 2007, wildfires hit San Diego. Messina asked a friend to tag his updates #sandiegofire, and others quickly used the same tag. In 2014, "hashtag" entered the Oxford English Dictionary.

Sources: CNBC (2018); The Register (2014), citing the OED; 99% Invisible, "Octothorpe" (2014).
