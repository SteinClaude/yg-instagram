## YS39 · Why is the power button a 1 and a 0?

Pakket: `pakketten/2026-12-15-YS39-aan-uit/` (video.mp4 25,7 s, overzicht.jpg, studio.txt, bron/bronnen.md, instagram/)

**Ingesproken tekst:** Your power button hides two numbers. A one, and a zero. In binary terms, one means on. Zero means off. Push them together, and you get this. The symbols are defined in an international standard: IEC 60417. For years they were only pictures. In 2015, Unicode accepted them as text. On and off, in one symbol. Press it, and it all starts again.

**Bronnen:**
- Kurt Kohlstedt, "Modern Hieroglyphs: Binary Logic Behind the Universal 'Power Symbol'", 99% Invisible (April 24, 2017): https://99percentinvisible.org/article/modern-hieroglyphics-binary-logic-behind-universal-power-symbol/
- Terence Eden, Joe Loughry and Bruce Nordman, "Proposal to Include IEC Power Symbols", Unicode L2/14-009R (February 14, 2014): https://www.unicode.org/L2/L2014/14009r-power-symbol.pdf

**Bewust niet gezegd:** Niet gezegd: het verhaal over schakelaars in de Tweede Wereldoorlog (geen bron), het jaar 1973 (alleen Wikipedia), de nummers per symbool, en dat de commissie de 1 en 0 zo bedoelde (de video zegt "in binary", zoals 99% Invisible). Knop, lampjes, norm en toets zijn eigen tekenwerk.

**Instagram-bijschrift:**

```
Why is the power button a 1 and a 0?

The power symbol combines a 1 and a 0: "on" and "off" in binary terms. The symbols are defined in an international standard, IEC 60417.

For years they existed only as pictures. In 2015, a proposal was accepted to add them to Unicode, so they could finally be typed and searched as text.

Sources: 99% Invisible (2017); Unicode proposal L2/14-009R (2014).

New every day at 18:00 (Amsterdam).
```
