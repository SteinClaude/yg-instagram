## YL02 — Why do we have leap years? (video 16:00, 65–90 s)

Bronnen:
- Lyle Tavernier, "Doing the Math on Why We Have Leap Day", NASA JPL Edu (16 jan 2024): https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/edu/news/doing-the-math-on-why-we-have-leap-day/
- Royal Museums Greenwich, "Which years are leap years and can you have leap seconds?": https://www.rmg.co.uk/stories/time/which-years-are-leap-years-can-you-have-leap-seconds
- "The Roman Calendar", Encyclopaedia Romana, University of Chicago: https://penelope.uchicago.edu/encyclopaedia_romana/calendar/romancalendar.html

Wat kan (met bron):
- "Earth takes about 365.2422 days to make one revolution around the Sun." · "about six hours longer than the 365 days" (NASA JPL)
- Julius Caesar: per 4 jaar één extra dag; van kracht 45 v.Chr. (U Chicago; RMG noemt 46 v.Chr. als instelling → jaartal niet uitspreken)
- Juliaans jaar "a bit more than eleven minutes too long, gaining almost a day every 128 years"; ±10 dagen verschoven in de jaren 1500 (U Chicago, deels samengevat)
- 1582: "October 4 was to be followed by October 15" (U Chicago) — weekdagen NIET noemen (geen bron)
- Eeuwregel: "divisible by 100 don't have leap days unless they're also divisible by 400" (NASA JPL); "the year 2000 was a leap year, although 1900 was not" (RMG)
- Groot-Brittannië 1752: "2 September was followed by 14 September" (RMG), 11 dagen
- Slot: "After only a century without leap day, summer wouldn't start until mid-July!" (NASA JPL)
- Niet: "overcorrected" (het Juliaanse jaar was te lang), "24 dagen per eeuw" als feit, "leaplings".

Wat kan: kappers deden ook "bloodletting, pulled teeth" (TheCollector); de paal komt volgens de overlevering van de staf die patiënten vastgrepen ("thought to"); de bebloede doeken wapperden om de paal: **legende, zo brengen** ("the story goes"); rood = bloed, wit = verband volgens veel verhalen (bronnen spreken elkaar deels tegen); 1745 scheiding in Engeland (Barbers' Company). Blauw: onzeker, weglaten of "no one knows for sure".

## Short — Why do onions make you cry?

Bronnen:
- Jason Daley, "This Enzyme Is Why Onions Make You Cry", Smithsonian Magazine (23 aug 2017): https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/researchers-finally-figure-out-onion-enzyme-makes-you-cry-180964325/
- Poison Control (National Capital Poison Center), "Why do onions make you cry?": https://www.poison.org/articles/why-do-onions-make-you-cry

Wat kan: snijden laat cellen knappen en "alliinase" vrijkomen (beide); een tweede enzym "lachrymatory factor synthase" maakt een vluchtige stof (Smithsonian); die "wafts into the air and reacts with the nerves of your cornea, causing uncontrollable tears" (Smithsonian); "chemical warfare used by the plants to ward off would-be predators" (Smithsonian); tip: 30 minuten koelen (Poison Control, National Onion Association), wortel laten zitten (Poison Control).
Niet: "syn-", "traanklieren spoelen", scherp mes / stromend water.

## Short — Why are flamingos pink?

Bronnen:
- Mehgan Murphy, "Why Are Flamingos Pink? And Other Flamingo Facts", Smithsonian's National Zoo (2021, bijgewerkt 2025): https://nationalzoo.si.edu/animals/news/why-are-flamingos-pink-and-other-flamingo-facts
- San Diego Zoo, "Flamingo": https://animals.sandiegozoo.org/animals/flamingo

Wat kan: kuikens "hatch with white-gray, downy feathers"; roze komt van "carotenoids" in "algae and brine shrimp"; "Flamingos really are what they eat" (National Zoo); eten met de kop ondersteboven, snavel als zeef (beide); dierentuinvoer bevat "a pigment that helps keep them 'in the pink'" (San Diego Zoo); ouders die jongen voeren verbleken tot "a pale pink or white" (San Diego Zoo).
Niet: lever, "canthaxanthin", "net als in wortels".

## Short — Why are pencils yellow?

Bronnen:
- Joe Schwarcz, "Pencils never contained lead", McGill Office for Science and Society (22 feb 2017): https://www.mcgill.ca/oss/article/did-you-know/pencils-never-contained-lead
- Caroline Weaver, "The Geography of Pencil History: Czech Republic", CW Pencils (2018): https://www.cwpencils.com/blog-archive/the-geography-of-pencil-history-czech-republic
- Paul Anthony Jones, "Why Are Pencils Yellow?", Mental Floss (2026): https://www.mentalfloss.com/history/why-pencils-are-yellow

Wat kan: de beste grafiet zou uit China komen (beide, "believed"); geel stond daar voor koninklijk/respect (Weaver: "originally chose the color yellow because of its connotations of royalty and respect in Chinese culture"); 1889 wereldtentoonstelling Parijs (Weaver, Mental Floss — niet 1890); vernoemd naar de Koh-i-Noor-diamant (Weaver); anderen namen geel over (beide); "lead pencils do not contain any lead" — grafiet en klei (McGill).
Niet: "75 %".
